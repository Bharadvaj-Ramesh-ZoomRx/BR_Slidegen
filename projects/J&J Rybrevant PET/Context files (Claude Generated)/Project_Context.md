## 1. Study Design

### TP1 — Immediate Recall
HCPs who self-report a recent sales rep interaction qualify. A single HCP can report multiple interactions, so the **base unit is interactions** (not unique HCPs). Captures:
- **Interaction details:** length, setting (office vs. outside), format (1:1 vs. group), who led (rep vs. HCP), structure (monologue vs. dialogue)
- **Content:** topic recall, message recall, order of message delivery, new information shared, unaided message recall
- **Rep performance:** rated across 10+ attributes — overall quality, preparedness, organized, tailored, product knowledge, indication knowledge, competitor knowledge, addressing unmet needs, answering questions, compelling reason to prescribe
- **Impact:** improvement in product perception post-interaction; LTIP (likelihood to increase prescription for RYB+LAZ for a specific indication/patient type) — this is the primary outcome metric

### TP2 — Delayed Recall
Any TP1 participant is eligible. **Base unit is unique HCPs** (not interactions) — sample is smaller than TP1. Captures:
- **Message effectiveness:** motivation, believability, differentiation
- **Message association**
- **Broader market perceptions:** product attribute ratings, HCP comfort, treatment positioning

### Sample & Cadence
- Quarterly fielding
- RYB target: ~100 interactions/wave; TAG target: ~60 interactions/wave
- TP2 sample smaller (unique HCP level, not interaction level)
- Q1 2026: survey launched January 2026 with updated screener and full FASPRO message set

### HCP Segments
| Segment | Definition |
|---------|-----------|
| Practice Setting | Academic (teaching hospitals, cancer centers, academic medical centers) vs. Community (office-based, community oncology practices) |
| Rep Impact Level | High Impact Interaction (HII): top box overall call quality AND top 2 box LTIP — both conditions required |
| Overlapper / Non-overlapper | Seen both RYB and TAG rep interaction vs. only one brand |
| MARIPOSA discussed 1st / Not | Used to assess impact of message delivery order on outcomes |
| New user of RYB+LAZ | **New Q1 2026** — tracking HCPs newly initiating RYB+LAZ prescriptions |

### Non-Rep Promotion (NPP)
- Rep emails are the most powerful NPP channel

- Client suggestion: test a district using only rep emails (no automated HQ emails) to isolate impact
